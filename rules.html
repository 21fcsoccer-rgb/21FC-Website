Please confirm and read all information, rules, and guidelines below before joining the group or registering for a game. These rules, along with the Membership Terms, govern every 21FC game.

Membership Requirement

You must be an approved 21FC member to sign up for any game.

Cancellations

  • All cancellations must be made through the OpenSports app.
  • The cancellation deadline is 48 hours before kickoff.
  • If you cancel or drop with less than 48 hours' notice, no refund or credit is given back, and an additional $20 cancellation fee is owed. $20 Fee
  • When a spot opens up, players on the OpenSports waitlist are added in order.

No-Shows & Late Arrivals

  • A "no-show" receives a straight Red Card: a $20 fee, and your account is disabled from reserving future games. $20 Fee
  • A Red Card means a two-week ban at minimum, and possible full removal from the group at the organizer's discretion.
  • Arriving after 7:15 AM is treated the same as a no-show, including the $20 fee. $20 Fee

On the Field

  • No cleats. Turf shoes or indoor shoes only.
  • No slide tackling. No fouls or fights. Two warnings and you're removed from the group.
  • We play without a referee — call your own fouls and honor the call.
  • Additional in-game rules will be explained at the field.

Liability Waiver & Agreement

Soccer is a physical sport that carries inherent risk of injury and/or accidents. By participating and attending, you acknowledge and accept that risk.

By agreeing to play, you fully understand that the organizer of these events holds zero liability for any injuries or accidents that may occur, and you confirm and attest that you are playing entirely at your own will and risk.

Additional

  • These rules, guidelines, and fees are subject to change and can be updated at any time by the organizer.
  • You may not solicit or post outside games, events, or business-related information in the group without approval first.
  • Questions, concerns, or feedback are always welcome — reach out directly any time.
