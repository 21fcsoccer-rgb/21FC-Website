Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What We Collect

When you request access to 21FC or reach out through this site, we may collect basic contact information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, or Instagram handle, typically through direct messages, email, or OpenSports.

How We Use It

  • To review and respond to requests to join 21FC
  • To communicate about schedules, memberships, and club updates
  • To manage referrals and membership benefits

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell or rent your information to third parties.
  • We don't share your contact information outside of what's needed to run the club (e.g., OpenSports for game registration).

Third-Party Services

Game registration and payments are handled through OpenSports, which has its own privacy practices. Instagram messages are subject to Instagram's own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to 21fc.soccer@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time as the club grows. Check back here for the current version.
